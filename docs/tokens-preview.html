📝 Semantic Content & Contrast Tiers

Text and icon tokens calibrated for accessible hierarchy and legibility.

Primary Content: High emphasis text for titles, headings, and primary body copy.
--fds-color-content-primary
Secondary Content: Medium emphasis text for descriptions, subheadings, and supporting copy.
--fds-color-content-secondary
Tertiary Content: Low emphasis placeholders, timestamps, and subtle hints.
--fds-color-content-tertiary
Disabled Content: Inactive labels and controls that cannot currently be interacted with.
--fds-color-content-disabled
Brand Content: Accentuated brand headlines and high-visibility links.
--fds-color-content-brand
